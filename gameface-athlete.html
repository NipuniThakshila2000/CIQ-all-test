GameFace

A simple check-in that helps people understand how they are doing.

GameFace is for people who are under pressure and the teams who support them. It turns a short check-in into a clear picture: what feels normal, what may need attention, and what to do next.

From Unknown To Known

Most people say they are fine until pressure shows up somewhere else.

GameFace is not meant to feel like homework. It starts with a simple truth: people often carry stress quietly. The product helps name what is happening early, in language anyone can understand.

Before

“I think I’m okay.”

The person may be tired, tense, distracted, or overloaded, but the signal is easy to miss.

During

A short check-in

GameFace asks natural questions and builds a simple picture from the response.

After

“Here’s what to do next.”

The result is a plain-language next step, not a page of science.

Who It Helps

GameFace belongs anywhere pressure affects performance, care, or judgment.

Athletes and teams: understand readiness, recovery, and when support may help.

Leaders and staff: notice strain earlier and check in before burnout becomes visible.

Care and support settings: keep the conversation human, simple, and action-focused.
