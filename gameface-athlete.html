Regulation breath

A slow exhale pattern - in for four, out for eight - to take the edge off the pressure signal.

4 min / Personal protocol

Attention reset

A single-point focus drill. Pick one fixed object at arm's length and hold it without scanning away.

1 min / Attention reset

Reframe

"Last match is data, not a debt." Stop the replay loop long enough for the rest to land.

Privacy / Consent / Control
Nothing is stored as raw identity.

GameFace is presented as a state-intelligence layer rather than a raw-identity archive.

Nothing reaches a coach, practitioner or programme without consent.

The athlete controls what is shared, who receives it and when that permission changes.
