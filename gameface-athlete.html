GameFace Athlete

Know where you are before deciding what to do.

This use case shows GameFace for sport and performance. It helps an athlete check stress, recovery, and readiness without turning the experience into a medical or technical report.

Simple Story

Performance is more than the scoreboard.

GameFace can begin with a short check-in. The athlete speaks naturally, reflects on how they feel, and receives a simple picture of pressure, recovery, and readiness.

The goal is not to overwhelm the athlete with numbers. The goal is to help them understand what is happening and what may help next.

Athlete Flow

From pressure to practical next steps.

The athlete gets a plain-language view of readiness: what looks steady, what may need attention, and what changed from recent check-ins.

The system can show recovery, struggle, flow, or release as a simple state rather than a dense technical dashboard.

Sharing stays athlete-controlled. Coaches, practitioners, or support circles should only receive what the athlete has agreed to share.
