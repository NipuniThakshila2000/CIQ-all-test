Civic Trust Use Case

Help people feel heard before decisions are made.

CIQ helps public teams understand what people may worry about, misunderstand, or reject before a message goes public.

The idea is simple: when people feel ignored, trust breaks. Civic Trust helps leaders listen earlier, speak more clearly, and avoid preventable backlash.

A simple way to prepare better public decisions.

02

03

Why It Matters

Good ideas can still fail if people feel left out.

A public decision is not only about facts. People also ask whether it is fair, whether they were respected, and whether anyone listened.

CIQ helps answer plain questions:

  • Will this make people feel heard?
  • What could be misunderstood?
  • Who needs a conversation first?

Where It Helps

Use it before the room turns against the message.

Community Dialogue

Prepare for meetings, consultations, and local conversations with clearer language.

Trust Repair

Spot where people may feel dismissed, blamed, or left out.

Policy Planning

Check a proposal before launch so the rollout is easier to understand.

Bottom Line

Less confusion. Better listening. More trust.

Civic Trust is for leaders who need to explain decisions to real people, not technical audiences. It keeps the focus on clarity, respect, and public confidence.
